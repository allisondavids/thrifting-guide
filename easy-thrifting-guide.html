Easy Thrifting Guide for Beginners

Your first thrift trip does not have to be intimidating. Follow these simple steps to make the experience easier and more successful.

1Know What You Are Looking For

Before you leave home, think about what you actually need. You might be looking for jeans, sweaters, workout clothes, home decor, or a specific color. Having a general goal keeps you from buying something simply because it is inexpensive.

2Dress for the Trip

Wear comfortable clothes and shoes because you may spend a while walking through racks. If you plan to try on clothing, an easy-to-remove layer can make changing easier. Bring a reusable bag if the store allows customers to use one.

3Start With a Quick Scan

Do not feel like you have to inspect every single item. Walk through the sections that match your needs and quickly scan for colors, fabrics, patterns, and styles that catch your attention. Once you find something interesting, take a closer look.

4Inspect Before You Buy

Check clothing for stains, holes, missing buttons, broken zippers, stretched elastic, and strong odors. Look at seams and hems as well. A low price is not a good deal if the item cannot realistically be worn or repaired.

5Try It On When Possible

Sizing can vary significantly between brands and decades. If fitting rooms are available, try the item on and move around in it. If there are no fitting rooms, compare the measurements with something you already own when possible.

6Ask Yourself One Question

Before purchasing, ask: "Would I still want this if it were not a bargain?" If the answer is no, put it back. Thrifting works best when you find pieces you genuinely like rather than collecting things because they are cheap.

Beginner's Thrift Trip Checklist

  • Set a spending limit before you shop.
  • Bring a list of items you actually need.
  • Wear comfortable clothes and shoes.
  • Inspect every item before purchasing.
  • Check the size and fit carefully.
  • Ask whether you would actually wear or use the item.
  • Leave room in your budget for a truly great find.
